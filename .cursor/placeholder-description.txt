Tower Rush is an offline modular-tower puzzle: you tap a slab, rotate it, and drop it onto a six-by-eight grid to raise the current blueprint. Play is skill over luck, because you pick the piece, the rotation, and the column, while the tray is filled from a fixed stage seed, and every overhang lowers stability until the tower collapses or no legal drop is left. You clear a stage by locking enough full rows to reach its floor target across twelve blueprints, from Foundation Block up to Apex Beacon. Each locked course pays twenty credits, a finished blueprint adds a one-hundred-credit bonus, and milestones unlock new slab themes. Credits stay on the in-game balance and cannot be cashed out, and the offline build includes no in-app purchases.
